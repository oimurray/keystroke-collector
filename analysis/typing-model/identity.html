How the check works

Each window becomes eight hold-time and transition-time summaries. Its score is the mean distance to the expected author’s five nearest normal training windows.

  • Key hold: median, interquartile range (IQR), and 90th percentile
  • Press to press: median, IQR, and 90th percentile
  • Release to next press: median and IQR

Can we tell who is typing just from the rhythm of their keystrokes? In this project, you answer this question on a concrete case: detecting that someone else has taken over a conversation with an LLM assistant in a web browser. You build the whole chain yourselves: a collector (for instance a browser extension) that records key presses and releases while chatting with an LLM, a dataset collected by the class from simple, harmless conversations, an exploration of these data, a similarity measure between pieces of free text, a frugal detector (for instance k nearest neighbours), and an honest evaluation of its errors as a function of the amount of text typed. The report is written like a short research paper: context, hypothesis and objectives, method, experiments, conclusion and discussion.
